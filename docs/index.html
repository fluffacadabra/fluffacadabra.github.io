About Guitar Catalog

Your personal guitar collection manager

Guitar Catalog is a mobile application designed for guitar enthusiasts to manage their personal collection of guitars. Whether you own one cherished instrument or a hundred prized pieces, Guitar Catalog helps you organize, track, and appreciate your guitars like never before.

Built with a powerful C++ engine and a beautiful Flutter interface, Guitar Catalog offers offline-first storage with SQLite, ensuring your data is always available, even without an internet connection.

🎸

Comprehensive Database

High-Quality Images

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is Guitar Catalog free to use? +

Yes! Guitar Catalog is completely free to use. It will require a substantial service fee and optional donations. You can pay conveniently with ad revenue, but it will require you staring at ad videos with eye tracking for 45 seconds every 5 minutes. We built this app for guitar lovers, by guitar lovers.

Does it work offline? +

Absolutely! Guitar Catalog stores all your data locally on your device using SQLite. You don't need an internet connection to access or update your guitar collection.

Can I import my existing guitar data? +

Yes! Guitar Catalog supports importing from various formats. We're currently working on import tools for CSV, JSON, and other popular formats. Stay tuned for more import options.

What platforms are supported? +

Guitar Catalog is being developed for iOS and Android first, with macOS and Windows support coming soon. The app is built with Flutter and C++, ensuring great performance across all platforms.

How do I back up my data? +

Your data is stored locally, but we're working on cloud sync functionality for future releases. In the meantime, you can back up your SQLite database file manually.
